Roll-up 1 — Intersect

WHAT CHANGED IN V2

  • New headline, broken so both halves of the turn land on their own line.
  • New sub-line. Note that “Palestinian” disappeared with the old one.
  • Five offers, no explanatory tails. The type grew from 43 mm to 50 mm, which is the real gain: these read from further back now.
  • Your QR is in, printing live at 178 mm square. The v1 block was a placeholder.
  • “Connect with us” replaces “Come and see”, and every instance of “Intersect Innovation Hub” is gone.

WHICH TO PICK

  • V1 if the banner has to explain what Intersect does to someone who has never heard of you. The tails do that work.
  • V2 if it stands where people already know you, or will ask. Bigger type, less to read, far stronger at four metres.
  • V2 is the better fit for OCIF Live itself. That audience is already inside the ecosystem.
Three things to settle before this prints. “Freelances” is set exactly as you wrote it. If you meant “Freelancers”, now is the moment. “Capacity building and training” and “Training and mentorship” both carry the word training, which reads as a stutter at this size; dropping it from the first would fix it. And the capital W in “What’s next” mid-sentence: deliberate, or should it be lowercase?

Roll-up 2 — OCIF Live

WHAT CHANGED IN V2

  • Three bullets, titles only. They now set at 56 mm instead of 43 mm.
  • QR removed, and the freed width went to the €50,000 block. The number grew from 76 mm to 110 mm.
  • Real date and time, given the weight the QR used to carry. The time sits in orange so it separates at a glance.
  • Venue reduced to Mariam Foundation, Ramallah. Carmel Hotel gone.
  • Em dashes removed throughout, including from the €50,000 line and the sub-line.

WORTH KNOWING

  • With no QR there is nothing to act on. If registration is still open on the day, or you want follow-up, that is a real loss.
  • The three bare bullets say what the session offers but not how. Anyone who does not already know OCIF will not learn much from them.
  • Date and time now sit inside the reading band, which is where a one-hour session most needs them.
  • The time range uses an en dash, the correct mark for a span and not an em dash. Say the word and it becomes a hyphen.

Print specification, all four

Standard roll-up hardware. Confirm against the actual cassette before artwork starts, since models vary by 20 to 30 mm and the difference is visible.

  • Finished size 850 × 2000 mm. Also holds at 800 × 2000 and 1000 × 2000.
  • Bleed 10 mm left and right, 30 mm top for the rail, 150 mm extra at the bottom to wrap into the cassette.
  • Safe area 50 mm in from each edge. All type sits inside the dashed line.
  • Dead zone Bottom 100 mm disappears into the base.
  • Resolution 150 dpi at full size, or vector throughout. The partner strip is upscaled from a web image and would print soft, so vector originals are needed. Same for the QR: the file you sent is a 656 px JPEG, fine on screen, but at 178 mm printed it wants vector or at least 1050 px square.
  • Colour CMYK, with the brand orange and magenta specified as Pantone if the printer supports it. Both shift badly in four-colour process.
  • Fonts Montserrat is standing in for the real brand faces. Swap, then outline before sending.
